How it works

Official records in, checked forecasts out, every month, without anyone touching it. Here is each step, and the choices behind it.

The monthly run

A scheduled GitHub Actions job runs these six steps on the 3rd of every month.

Download

Fetch the newest sales and Ejari lease records from the Dubai Land Department open data service.

Check

Run data checks: volumes, missing fields, impossible values, duplicates, sudden jumps. Any failure stops the run.

Measure

Turn deals into a monthly typical price and rent per sq ft for each area, home type and size group.

Retrain

Retrain the model on all history, recalibrate its ranges on the last 12 months, and check it has not got worse.

Score

Compare every past forecast with the month that has just been recorded, and add it to the public track record.

Publish

Save this month's forecast unchanged in the archive and republish this website.

What is forecast

Two things, 12 months ahead: the yearly rent per sq ft of new lease contracts (renewals follow rent-cap rules and lag the market), and the sale price per sq ft of residential sales, ready and off-plan. Each is forecast for every area, for apartments and villas or townhouses, and for studios to 4+ bedrooms grouped by floor area (rent records rarely list bedrooms, so the size limits are set from sales that do), wherever there are at least 30 deals in the last 3 months and 3 years of history.

The typical level

For each month we drop the 5% most extreme deals on each side and take the average of the rest on a log scale (a geometric mean), pooled over 3 months. It behaves like a median but can be built up exactly from monthly totals, so the history never needs the raw records again.

The model

One gradient-boosted model (LightGBM) learns from all areas at once, which helps smaller areas borrow strength from larger ones. Its inputs are only things known on the forecast date: recent momentum of the area and of Dubai, how volatile it is, the number of deals and how it is changing, the gap between rent and price (the yield), the share of off-plan sales, and the season.

The ranges

The model predicts five points of the likely outcome (10%, 25%, 50%, 75%, 90%). The ranges are then widened or narrowed using the last 12 months of real outcomes (conformal calibration), so that recently 8 in 10 outcomes fell inside the 8-in-10 range.

The benchmarks

Four simpler methods are scored the same way: no change, last year's trend continuing, the Dubai-wide trend applied everywhere, and classic exponential smoothing per series. The model has to beat them on the test years to be worth using. See how it does.

Limits

Typical price per sq ft moves when the mix of buildings traded changes, not only when prices change. Area names follow the Land Department's official areas, which can cover several communities. Forecasts cannot see shocks coming. This is not financial advice.

Data and licence
