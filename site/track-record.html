Are these forecasts any good?

We replayed the past. At regular points since 2015, the model saw only the data available at that time, forecast the next 12 months, and was scored against what really happened. Forecasts made from 2023 on were kept aside until every design choice was frozen, so they are a fair test.

How the test works

At each test date the model is retrained from scratch on the data known that month, exactly as the monthly job does today. It never sees later months. The error is the gap between the forecast typical price per sq ft and the real one, as a percentage of the real one. One simplification: past months come from today's files, which include a few records registered late.

What it cannot do

Forecasts follow momentum, the local supply of deals and the gap between rents and prices, so they are late at turning points. Forecasts made in 2017 and 2018 expected rents 10% to 20% higher than they turned out, as rents fell. Forecasts made from October 2020 to April 2021 came in 10% to 19% below the real rents and prices, as the boom began. A sudden shock will show up as a miss here, and in the live record.
