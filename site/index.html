    What "typical" means

    For each area, home type and bedroom count, we take every registered deal of the last 3 months, drop the 5% most extreme on each side and average the price (or yearly rent) per sq ft. A typical home is that figure times the typical size of homes traded there.

    How sure is this?

    Every forecast comes with a range. The darker band is where the real figure should land half the time, the lighter band 8 times in 10. We check this against what really happened, every month.
